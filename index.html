Dark Crawl
Press the VR goggles button (bottom right) to play in VR. You hang low like a gorilla: reach down, push off surfaces with your hands, release to launch. Poke buttons with your hand.
In VR: shake the LEFT controller to power the torch, swing the RIGHT pickaxe to mine, hold X for admin controls: Chest ESP, Fast Mine, Fly, Noclip, God Mode, Full Bright.
On PC: drag to look, WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, click to poke or mine the block under the dot, hold F to shake the torch, hold X for admin controls. Noclip: WASD + Space/Ctrl; Shift speeds up.
Save data (cosmetics) is stored in this browser.
