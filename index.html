Dark Crawl
Press the VR goggles button (bottom right) to play in VR. You hang low like a gorilla: reach down, push off surfaces with your hands, release to launch. Poke buttons with your hand.
Worlds: Crawl Space (mine + crafting table), The Forest (monsters, gun, flashlight), The Backrooms (find the exit).
Online: poke ONLINE on the stage menu, host to get a 4-digit code, friends join with it.
In VR: shake the LEFT controller to power the torch/flashlight, swing the RIGHT pickaxe to mine, RIGHT trigger shoots, hold X for the admin panel. With Fly: thumbsticks move you; with Platforms: hold a grip.
On PC: drag to look, WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, click to poke / mine / shoot, E mine, hold F to shake the light, hold X for the admin panel (then click it), Q = platform, Ctrl/C = down when flying.
Save data (cosmetics, name, inventory) is stored in this browser.
