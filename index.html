Dark Crawl
Press the VR goggles button (bottom right) to play in VR. You hang low like a gorilla: reach down, push off surfaces with your hands, release to launch. Poke buttons with your hand.
Worlds: Crawl Space (mine + crafting table), The Forest (monsters, gun, flashlight), The Backrooms (find the exit), The School (3 keys, ghosts freeze when watched), Only Up (a sky-high obby with checkpoints), Hills (hard mode: night comes fast, monster sheep, find 10 runes to open the secret door).
Online: poke ONLINE on the stage menu, host to get a 4-digit code, friends join with it. Or open PUBLIC LOBBIES to jump into one of 8 open rooms. Turn on voice chat in SETTINGS (browser will ask for the microphone; allow it before you enter VR).
In VR: shake the LEFT controller to power the torch/flashlight, swing the RIGHT pickaxe to mine, RIGHT trigger shoots, hold X for settings. With Fly: thumbsticks move you; with Platforms: hold a grip.
On PC: drag to look, WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, click to poke / mine / shoot, E mine, hold F to shake the light, hold X for settings (then click it) or poke SETTINGS on the menu, Q = platform, Ctrl/C = down when flying.
Save data (cosmetics, name, inventory) is stored in this browser.
